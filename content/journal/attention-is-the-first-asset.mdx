Every business I have worked on had the same first problem. The product could be good, the price fair, the owner capable, and none of it counted until the right person paid attention.

## Narrow beats loud

The instinct is to reach more people. The better move is usually to matter more to fewer of them. A message written for one kind of customer does more work than a message written for everyone.

## Consistency is a strategy

One channel, done properly and for long enough, beats five done in bursts. People trust what keeps showing up.

## Measure the thing you can change

Reach is easy to count and hard to act on. Replies, booked calls and repeat customers tell you what to do next.
