Deal summaries are written to be persuasive. Reading one well means going through it in your own order, not the author's.

## Start with the assumptions

Every projection rests on a handful of inputs: rent, vacancy, expenses, the cost of money, the exit. I find those first and ask where each one came from.

## Then the downside

What happens if rents stay flat, if a renovation runs long, if the loan resets higher? A good operator has already written that page.

## Then the people

Who is doing the work, what have they done before, and how are they paid? Alignment matters more than any single figure.

None of this is investment advice. It is how I read, and it is where a first conversation with me usually begins.
