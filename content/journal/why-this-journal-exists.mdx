I work in real estate, I invest, and I build marketing businesses. People often ask which one I really do. The honest answer is that each makes me better at the others.

Selling a home is a marketing problem. Investing is a decision about where attention and capital are going next. Building a business teaches you what a deadline costs.

This journal is where I write those connections down.
