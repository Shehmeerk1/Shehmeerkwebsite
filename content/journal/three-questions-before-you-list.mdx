A listing is a launch. The work that decides how it goes happens before anyone sees the house.

## Who is the likely buyer?

Not everyone. A specific kind of person, at a specific stage of life, who will see this house and feel it was meant for them. Every decision about preparation and photography follows from that answer.

## What would stop them?

Walk the house the way a stranger would. Deferred maintenance, a dark room, a confusing floor plan. Some of these are cheap to fix and some are better priced in than repaired. Sorting one from the other is most of the job.

## What is the story in one sentence?

If the house cannot be described in a sentence, the marketing will be a list of features. Features are what people compare. A story is what they remember.
