# Photography shot list

Every "Photo to come" note on the site, with its brief. One morning and one evening shoot covers most of it. Golden hour in Phoenix is roughly the hour before sunset; the homepage hero readout shows today's time.

| Page | Frame | Brief |
|---|---|---|
| Home, hero | Shehmeer, golden hour | Waist-up, facing the light, desert behind. Warm, direct, no studio. 3:4 portrait. |
| Home, introduction | Shehmeer at work | Candid, mid-conversation at a showing or over plans. Natural light, 35mm, not posed. 4:5. |
| About, hero | Portrait of Shehmeer | The lead portrait. Head and shoulders, eyes to camera, warm natural light, a plain wall. 3:4. |
| About, chapters | Shehmeer, early days | An older photograph from the first business, if one exists. 4:3. |
| About, chapters | Shehmeer with a team | Working with people: a meeting, a launch, a whiteboard. 4:3. |

| About, off the clock | A desert morning | A trail, a coffee, the hour before work. 4:5. |
| About, off the clock | Where the work happens | Desk, car, notebook: the honest version. 4:5. |
| Marketing, background | Shehmeer with a founder | Working session, whiteboard or laptop between them. Documentary, not staged. 4:5. |
| Marketing (optional) | Showreel | 60 seconds of real work. 16:9 video. There is no slot for it until it exists. |

Also worth replacing: the Arizona stand-ins (hero desert, Camelback, Taliesin) with your own landscape photography, at 2400px wide or more.

To place a photograph: add the file to `public/photos/`, register it in `lib/photos.ts`, and replace the `PhotoNote` with `<Photo name="..." />` (on the homepage hero and the identity scroll, put the portrait where the note sits).
